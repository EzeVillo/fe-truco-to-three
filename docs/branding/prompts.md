# Prompts de los maestros

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada (`image_gen`), con transparencia
exterior. Los textos siguientes son los prompts finales enviados a la
herramienta. El resultado se normalizó y exportó con el script del repositorio.

## Emblema completo

Referencias: último boceto con cartas tradicionales y los naipes existentes
`public/cards/1_espada.webp`, `1_basto.webp` y `7_espada.webp`.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production master image for the existing Argentine game TRUCO A 3.
Finish: professional restrained graphic identity, mathematically clean outlines, precisely drawn coherent ink lines, careful typesetting. Flat solid spot colors only. Brand forest green #062618, warm gold #e8b73a, ivory #e6e9e5. Traditional Spanish card illustrations can additionally use classic red blue yellow green.
No gradient or variation in any solid area. No metallic shine, no bevel, no shadow, no noise, no speckles, no paper grain, no glow, no drop shadow, no double contours. Crisp original artwork, not a photograph or mockup. No French poker suits, casino tokens or new decorative flourishes.
Transparent outside the emblem. All corners outside circular silhouette must have clean genuine alpha transparency with no colored fringes, no stray pixels, no white background. Keep the entire circle comfortably inside the square image, with 5% transparent margin at all edges. Perfectly square output as large and sharp as possible.
Input reference: image 1 is the selected near-final full-logo CONCEPT to clean and finalize; images 2, 3, 4 are authoritative playing-card references (ace of swords, ace of bastos, seven of swords). Preserve the reference composition closely: circular dark-green medallion with a SINGLE clean warm-gold ring, traditional fan of exactly three cards above large upright ivory slab-serif "TRUCO", then small ivory "A" and bold gold "3". Preserve the visual identity, proportions and clear lettering of image 1. Do not redesign the typography, enlarge ornaments or simplify the cards into pictograms.
Improve ONLY the artwork quality and precision: perfectly smooth circular perimeter with one consistent stroke, flat uniform dark-green background, clean unbroken card borders, coherent classic illustration, consistent ink weight, precise letter shapes and even spacing. Remove the stray red/yellow perimeter pixels and any jagged or doubled outside outlines seen in the concept.
The cards must be faithful to references 2–4: left card rank 1, ornate Spanish sword with decorated gold hilt and colored ribbons; center card rank 1, the traditional tapered yellow/red/green basto with small leaves; right card rank 7, exactly SEVEN slender blue-and-gold swords in the same 3 + 1 horizontal + 3 arrangement as reference. No additional swords, glyphs, ranks or invented anatomy. Upper printed indices exactly 1, 1, 7. Traditional thin dark card borders.
Use only one calm enclosing rounded green nameplate with flat gold outline around "TRUCO", as in selected reference. Remove redundant little gold dashes near the A / 3 for cleaner spacing; the text hierarchy should remain strong. The 3 stays completely inside the circle with generous padding.
Exact logo text only: "TRUCO", "A", "3". No heading, captions, variants, size samples, app icons or presentation board. One single clean finished emblem.
```
